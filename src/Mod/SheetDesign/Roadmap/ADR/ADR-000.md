
# ADR-000: Ubiquitous Language

## Context

Software doesn't cope well with ambiguity. In order to have productive conversation with other human developers or with AI coding agents, consistent use of common and rigorous language is needed. The concept of a 'Ubiquitous Language' is described in Eric Evan's book Domain Driven Design

Sheet Design serves more than one industry. Sheet metal and packaging use different words for the same geometry. This glossary gives one canonical term for each concept and records the domain words as synonyms.

## Decision

This ADR will contain the Ubiquitous Language Glossary.  It will be updated as needed.

## Language

### Scope and domains

**Sheet Design**:
The umbrella concept and the name of the module (`src/Mod/SheetDesign`, namespace `SheetDesign`). The design of parts that are made from one flat sheet of constant thickness by cuts and folds.
_Avoid_: "Sheet Metal" as the name of the module or of a generic concept (it is one **Domain**), "SheetMetalCore" (the earlier module name).

**Domain**:
An industry that uses Sheet Design with its own words, materials, and standard parts. Two Domains are established: **Sheet Metal** and **Packaging**. A Domain adds capabilities and vocabulary. It does not change the **Sheet Model**.

**Sheet Metal** (Domain):
Parts from metal sheet that a press brake or a folder bends. Its words: bend, flange, bend radius, K-factor, relief, hem.

**Packaging** (Domain):
Parts from corrugated or solid fiberboard that are scored and folded. Its words: score, flap, panel, slot, blank, scoring allowance. The reference for standard parts is the International Fibreboard Case Code (the box styles 02 to 09 of the Fibre Box Handbook).

**SheetMetal addon**:
The external workbench `FreeCAD_SheetMetal`. It is a different code base. Its user directory is `sheetmetal`, and FreeCAD lets a user directory replace a core module that has the same name in lowercase. This is one reason for the module name **Sheet Design**.
_Avoid_: "the old workbench", "SheetMetal" without "addon" when the addon is meant.

### The sheet and its rule

**Sheet**:
The material: flat, with one constant **Thickness**. A part is made from one Sheet.
_Avoid_: plate, board, stock (in code and UI).

**Sheet Rule**:
The object that holds the values for a full part. It is an `App::VarSet`. The properties `Thickness`, `BendRadius`, and `KFactor` are necessary. Optional properties: `KFactorStandard` (ANSI or DIN), `KFactorRatios` and `KFactorValues` (the **K-Factor Table**), and `ReliefType` (None, Rectangle, Round), `ReliefWidth`, and `ReliefDepth`. The relief properties: they give the **Relief** of each Flange that has the relief type **Rule**. A feature does not hold its own copy of the necessary values.
_Avoid_: gauge table, material sheet, defaults.

**Thickness**:
The thickness of the **Sheet**. In flat space the material is between z = 0 and z = Thickness.
_Packaging_: caliper.

**Bend Radius**:
The inner radius of a **Bend**. A score in fiberboard uses the radius 0.

**K-Factor**:
The position of the neutral axis as a fraction of the **Thickness**, measured from the inner surface of the bend (the ANSI convention). Range 0 to 1.

**K-Factor Table** (`KFactorRatios` and `KFactorValues` of the Sheet Rule):
Two lists of the same length that give a K-Factor for each ratio of Bend Radius to Thickness. A Bend uses the first ratio that is not smaller than its own ratio. Empty lists mean that all Bends use `KFactor`. `KFactorStandard` (ANSI or DIN) applies to the single value and to the table; a DIN value is two times the ANSI value.
_Avoid_: "material definition sheet", "MDS" (addon words).
_Note_: the table is on the Sheet Rule because the values change with the tools of a shop. A material card can later supply default values.

**Bend Allowance**:
The length of a **Bend Zone** in the **Flat Pattern**: `(BendRadius + KFactor × Thickness) × angle`, with the angle in radians.
_Packaging_: scoring allowance.
_Avoid_: bend deduction (a different quantity; not used).

### The model

**Sheet Model** (`SheetModel`):
The explicit description of a part: a tree of **Panels** that **Bends** join. The **Folded Solid** and the **Flat Pattern** are two outputs of the same Sheet Model. No operation makes a Flat Pattern from the faces of a solid that Sheet Design made.
_Avoid_: "the solid" when the model is meant.

**Panel**:
A flat region of the part. It has a profile (one face with its openings) in **Flat Space** and a **Folded Position**.
_Sheet Metal_: wall, face. _Packaging_: panel (side panel, end panel), flap.

**Root Panel**:
The Panel that does not move when the part folds. It gives the position of the **Flat Pattern**.

**Bend**:
A fold that joins a parent Panel to a child Panel along a straight line. It has an angle, a direction (up or down), and a radius.
_Packaging_: score, scoreline.
_Avoid_: fold as a noun for this concept (see the open questions), crease.

**Bend Line**:
The straight line of a Bend. In the model it is the edge of the parent Panel where the Bend starts. In a **Flat Pattern** and in the input of **Refold** it is the center line of the **Bend Zone**.

**Bend Zone**:
The region of the **Flat Pattern** that a Bend uses, inside a strip with the width of the **Bend Allowance**. It can have any outline and openings, and a cut can divide it into parts. A zone that is the full rectangle gives an exact solid of revolution. Each other zone is wrapped on the cylinders of the Bend.
_Note_: with the Bend Radius 0, a curved edge of a Bend Zone must go in one direction along the Bend Line. An opening inside the zone is not available, because the solid would touch itself on the bend axis.

**Flat Space**:
The plane z = 0 in which all Panel profiles and all Bend Zones are. All Panels use the same Flat Space, thus their profiles make the Flat Pattern without a transformation.

**Folded Position**:
The rigid transformation of a Panel from Flat Space to its place in the folded part.

**Layer**:
The position of a Panel in a stack of Panels that are one on the other, for example the flaps that close a box. Panels in the same Layer and the same area collide.

**Offset** (`Flange.Offset`):
A move of a Bend Line out of its parent Panel (positive) or into it (negative). An Offset of one Thickness puts a Panel in the next Layer.

**Overlap** (`Feature.Overlap`):
The volume of material that two or more Panels or Bends have in the same space. Zero for a part that can exist.
_Avoid_: collision count, interference (use Overlap for the measured value).

**Origin** (`Origin`):
The record of where a straight boundary segment of a Panel came from: the feature that made it and its role (for example the end of a Flange, or edge 3 of the base profile). A later cut or Offset changes the sequence of the edges of a Panel. It does not change the Origin.

**Tag**:
The text of an Origin or of a Panel, for example `Flange002_End` or `Base`. It starts with the name of the feature that made the element.

**Stable Name**:
The name that Sheet Design gives to an edge or a face of an output shape, from a Tag and a suffix: `_T` and `_B` for an edge on the top or bottom side of a Panel, `_S` for a side face, `_Top` and `_Bottom` for the faces of a Panel, `_F` for an edge of the Flat Pattern, `_BendLine` for a Bend Line. FreeCAD keeps a link to an element by this name, thus the link stays correct when the number of the element changes.
_Avoid_: "edge number" or "Edge12" as an identity (the number is not stable).

**Coupled Fold**:
A set of Bends around a point that can move only together, for example the web at the corner of a bellows box. The Panels around the point make a closed loop.

**Closing Bend** (`Bend.closing`):
The Bend that closes a loop of Panels. Its two Panels have their Folded Position from other Bends. The model checks that the angle of the Closing Bend agrees with those positions, and gives an error if the folds do not close. A Closing Bend has a Bend Zone in the Flat Pattern and no solid in the Folded Solid.
_Note_: the user gives all angles. The model does not calculate the angles of a Coupled Fold.

**Relief Opening**:
A small opening that **Refold** makes where two Bend Lines cross. The Bend Zones of the two lines end at the opening. The sheet cannot fold at such a point without it.

**Through Line**:
A Bend Line that does not end at a point where Bend Lines meet, or that a second line continues on the same axis. A Through Line stops the Bend Zones of the other lines at the point. A line that only ends at the point, for example the diagonal of a web, gets the region that the Through Lines leave.

### Outputs

**Folded Solid**:
The solid of the part in its folded state. It is the shape of a Sheet Design feature.

**Flat Pattern** (`SheetDesign::FlatPattern`):
The part in its flat state: one face and the Bend Lines. It follows its source feature. Its properties `BendAngles` and `BendRadii` give the data of each Bend, in the sequence of the Bend Lines. A positive angle is a Bend to the top side of the Root Panel (up).
_Packaging_: blank, box blank.
_Avoid_: unfolded shape, development, net.

**Export Layer**:
One of the five groups of a Flat Pattern in a DXF or an SVG file: `OUTLINE`, `CUTOUT` (openings), `BEND_UP`, `BEND_DOWN` (dashed center lines), and `BEND_TEXT`. A cutter must not cut a Bend Line, thus the layers are apart.

**Bend Label**:
The text of a Bend in an export: the direction, the angle, and the radius, for example `UP 90° R1`.

### Features and operations

**Base Panel** (`SheetDesign::BasePanel`):
The first Panel of a part, from a closed planar profile.

**Flange** (`SheetDesign::Flange`):
A Bend and a rectangular Panel on a straight edge of a Panel.
_Packaging_: flap.
_Avoid_: wall (the addon word).

**Flange Position** (`Flange.Position`):
Where a Flange is against the selected edge.
- **Material Outside**: the Bend starts at the edge. The part becomes longer by the Bend Radius and the Thickness.
- **Material Inside**: the outer face of the Flange is at the edge. The parent Panel becomes shorter.
- **Thickness Outside**: the inner face of the Flange is at the edge.

The **Offset** adds to the position.
_Note_: the values are exact for a Bend of 90 degrees. For other angles the Bend Line has the same position as for 90 degrees.

**Length Type** (`Flange.LengthType`):
How the `Length` of a Flange is measured.
- **Leg**: the flat part of the Flange, from the end of the Bend.
- **Outer Sharp**: from the line where the outer faces of the two Panels meet.
- **Inner Sharp**: from the line where the inner faces of the two Panels meet.
- **Tangential**: from the plane that touches the outer side of the Bend. For 90 degrees this is the full height of the Flange.

_Avoid_: "flange height" and "flange length" without the Length Type.

**Relief** (`Flange.ReliefType`, `ReliefWidth`, `ReliefDepth`):
A slot in the parent Panel at an end of a Bend, in the **Gap** of the Flange. It prevents a tear at the end of the Bend. Its type is **Rule** (the default: the values of the **Sheet Rule**), **None**, **Rectangle**, or **Round** (the inner end is a half circle). The width is along the edge and is not larger than the Gap. The depth is from the Bend Line into the Panel. An end with no Gap gets no Relief.
_Avoid_: "bend relief" for a **Relief Opening** (that is the opening where two Bend Lines cross in Refold), notch.

**Flange Profile** (`Flange.Profile`):
An optional sketch that gives a Flange its form. There are two kinds.
- **Shaped Flange**: a closed outline in the plane of the parent Panel, on the outer side of the edge. Its straight side on the edge gives the Bend Line. The outline is the flat part of the Flange.
- **Contour**: an open line of straight segments in a plane at 90 degrees to the edge. It starts on the edge, on the top or the bottom side of the Panel. Each segment gives one Bend and one Panel. The line is the outline of that side of the sheet with sharp corners, thus that side of the Folded Solid is on the line.

_Avoid_: "sketch wall", "multi-bend".

**Miter** (`Flange.Miter1`, `Flange.Miter2`):
The angle of a side of a Flange. The side starts at the Bend Line, thus the Bend Zone and the Panel of the Flange are narrower to the far end for a positive angle.

**Automatic Miter** (`Flange.AutoMiter`, `Flange.MiterGap`):
A cut of two Flanges on adjacent edges of one Panel that are in the same region of the Flat Pattern, for example at an inner corner. The cut is on the line through the point where the two Bend Lines meet, in the middle between the Flanges. It changes the new Flange and the earlier Flange. Two Flanges that are apart in the Flat Pattern stay as they are.
_Avoid_: "corner treatment" (not defined), "miter" for a **Relief**.

**Gap** (`Flange.Gap1`, `Flange.Gap2`):
The distance from an end of the selected edge to the start of the Flange.
_Packaging_: the Gaps of two flaps make a slot.

**Cut** (`SheetDesign::Cut`):
A removal of a profile through the Thickness. The profile is on a Panel (space **Folded**) or on the Flat Pattern (space **Flat**). A Cut across a Bend Zone must be in the space Flat, because a profile on a Panel has no clear position in the Bend.
_Packaging_: slot, die cut.

**Unfold** (`SheetDesign::Unfold`):
The operation that reads a folded solid from a different tool and gives its Sheet Model and its Flat Pattern. The Thickness and each Bend Radius come from the geometry.
_Avoid_: convert, flatten, "from solid".

**Refold** (`SheetDesign::Refold`):
The operation that folds a finished flat outline along Bend Lines and gives its Sheet Model and its Folded Solid.
_Avoid_: "from flat", fold up.

**Body** (PartDesign):
The container of a part. Each Sheet Design feature is a PartDesign feature, thus a Body accepts it and it can be the Tip. The commands put a new part into a Body. A PartDesign feature (for example a Pocket) can follow a Sheet Design feature. It changes the solid, and it has no **Sheet Model**. To continue with Sheet Design features after it, use **Unfold** on its solid.
_Avoid_: "sheet metal body", "sheet body".

**Resource library** (`SheetDesignGui`):
The C++ library of the GUI side. It holds only the compiled resources: the icons, and the `.ui` files of the task panels. All GUI logic is in Python, as in the CAM workbench.
_Avoid_: GUI logic in C++.

**Task Panel**:
The edit dialog of a feature. It has two parts: a `.ui` file in the Resource library (`Gui/Resources/panels`), and a Python class in `SheetDesignTaskPanels.py`. The base class connects each widget to the property of the feature that has the same name as the widget. A new value thus needs a property on the feature and a widget with that name in the `.ui` file.
_Note_: OK keeps the changes. Cancel puts all values back.

**View Provider Proxy** (`SheetDesignViewProviders.ViewProvider`):
The Python object that gives a feature its icon and opens its Task Panel. The commands attach it to each new feature.

**Edge check** (`SheetDesign.edgeInfo`):
The function that tells Python code if a Flange is possible on an edge or a side face of a feature. A Task Panel uses it to limit the selection.

**Entry**:
One of the three ways to make a Sheet Model: build it (Base Panel and Flanges), **Refold** it, or **Unfold** it. After each Entry the same features can follow.

### Terms from the domains that are not implemented

**Hem**: an edge that is folded back on itself. Sheet Metal.
**Manufacturer's Joint**: the place where the two ends of a box blank are fastened, often with a glue tab. Packaging.
**Box Style**: a standard part of the International Fibreboard Case Code, for example the Regular Slotted Container (0201). Packaging. The module has a **Box Style Catalog** (`SheetDesignBoxStyles.py`): one parametric blank for each style, as the input of **Refold**. The blanks are an interpretation of the handbook drawings, which have no dimensions.
**Piece**: one flat part of a Box Style. A style can have more than one Piece, for example a body and a cover. Packaging.
**Layer number**: in the Box Style Catalog, the count of thicknesses by which the score of a flap is moved out, thus the flap is in that **Layer**.

## Relationships

- A part has exactly one **Sheet Rule** and one **Sheet Model**.
- A **Sheet Model** has one **Root Panel**, one or more **Panels**, and one **Bend** for each Panel that is not the Root Panel.
- A **Bend** joins exactly one parent **Panel** to one child **Panel**. The Bends that give the Panels their position make a tree. A **Closing Bend** joins two Panels of that tree and closes a loop.
- Each feature holds the **Sheet Model** that results from its operation. Its shape is the **Folded Solid** of that model.
- A **Flat Pattern** has exactly one source feature and reads the **Sheet Model** of that feature.
- An **Entry** starts a part. **Flange** and **Cut** continue a part from each Entry.
- Each straight boundary segment of a **Panel** has at most one **Origin**. Each named element of an output shape has exactly one **Stable Name**.
- A feature that is suppressed gives the **Sheet Model** of the feature before it.
- A **Flat Pattern** is not in the **Body** of its source. It is in the coordinates of that Body.
- A **Domain** gives vocabulary and capabilities. It does not own Panels, Bends, or outputs.

## Example dialogue

> **Packaging designer:** "I want a Regular Slotted Container. Do I draw the blank?"
> **Maintainer:** "You can. Draw the outline and the Bend Lines, then **Refold**. Or build it: a **Base Panel** for one side panel and a **Flange** for each other panel and each flap. The **Flat Pattern** then gives you the blank."
>
> **Packaging designer:** "The outer flaps go through the inner flaps."
> **Maintainer:** "They are in the same **Layer**. Look at **Overlap** on the last feature. Give the outer flaps an **Offset** of one **Thickness**."
>
> **Sheet metal designer:** "I have a STEP file of a bracket. How do I get the flat?"
> **Maintainer:** "**Unfold** it. Select one flat face. The K-Factor comes from the **Sheet Rule**; the Thickness and the radii come from the solid."

## Open questions

1. **Unfold and Refold.** This ADR uses the two words for the two Entries from other data (a folded solid, a flat outline). Commercial systems use the same pair for a temporary change of state in one part: unfold some bends, add a feature, refold. Which meaning is canonical?  Discuss this with me.
2. **Bend or Fold, Flange or Flap.** The code uses the Sheet Metal words `Bend` and `Flange` as the canonical terms and records the Packaging words as synonyms. Is that correct, or must the canonical terms be neutral?  That is correct.
3. **How a Domain shows in the product.** Options: one workbench with domain toolbars, one workbench for each Domain on the same module, or only templates and vocabulary. Only templates and vocabulary
4. **Blank.** Is "blank" an accepted Packaging synonym in the UI, or does the UI always say Flat Pattern?  In the UI use 'Flat Pattern' it is more descriptive.
